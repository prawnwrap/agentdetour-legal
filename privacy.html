Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026 — Agent Detour, operated by Bullit Technologies Limited

This Privacy Policy explains how Bullit Technologies Limited ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Agent Detour mobile app ("the App").

Agent Detour is free to download and use, and does not display advertising. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we never will.

1. Who we are

Agent Detour is operated by Bullit Technologies Limited, a company registered in the United Kingdom. If you have questions about this policy or your data, you can contact us at privacy@agentdetour.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: a username you choose, and optionally an email address if you use a login method that requires one.
  • Preferences: the sports, competitions, and teams you follow, so we can personalise your feed.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier that lets us send you notifications you've opted into (e.g. breaking news, kickoff reminders). This is managed via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Usage data: which stories, games, and features you interact with, used to improve content relevance and to detect trending topics.
  • Mates/social features: if you use the "mates" or friend-search functionality, we store your username and the connections you make. Search is limited to protect your privacy — see Section 5.
  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system version, and app version, used for compatibility and troubleshooting.
We do not currently collect crash or session-replay diagnostic data. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to reflect it before that data collection begins.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and personalise the App's core features (your feed, scores, games, and notifications)
  • To operate social features you choose to use (following mates, leaderboards)
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and performance of the App
  • To communicate with you about the App, if you contact us or opt into notifications

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

4. Who we share information with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to operate the App. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — database and backend infrastructure hosting your account and preference data
  • Firebase (Google) — push notification delivery
  • OpenAI — used to generate story summaries and breakdowns from publicly available sports news content. This does not involve sending your personal account data to OpenAI
  • API-Sports and other sports data providers — supply live scores and statistics; we do not send your personal data to these providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Mates and search

To protect your privacy, single-word searches only match against usernames, not real names. Multi-word searches may also match first/last names if you've chosen to share them. You control what's visible to other users in your settings.

6. Data retention

We retain account and preference data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the App's settings, which will remove your personal data from our systems within 30 days, except where we're required to retain it for legal or security purposes.

7. Your rights

If you are located in the UK or EU, you have rights under UK GDPR / EU GDPR, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@agentdetour.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Children's privacy

Agent Detour is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Future changes — subscriptions

Agent Detour is currently free with no advertising. If we introduce an optional paid subscription in future, this policy will be updated to describe any additional payment-related data (handled by our payment processor, not stored directly by us) before that feature launches.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes we'll notify you within the App.
