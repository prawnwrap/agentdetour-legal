Delete Your Account

Agent Detour — operated by Bullit Technologies Limited

How to delete your account

You can permanently delete your Agent Detour account and all associated data directly within the app:

  1. Open the Agent Detour app and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete My Account
  4. Confirm when prompted — this is permanent and cannot be undone

Your account is deleted immediately and you'll be signed out automatically. There is no waiting period.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to sign in or no longer have the app installed, email privacy@agentdetour.com from the email address associated with your account, and we'll delete your account and data on your behalf within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently and immediately removed:

  • Your profile, username, and account credentials
  • Your sports, competition, and team preferences
  • Your saved/bookmarked stories
  • Your search history
  • Your game scores, streaks, and leaderboard positions
  • Your mate connections (followers and following)
  • Your notification settings and push notification token

Nothing is retained after deletion, and the deletion cannot be reversed.

Analytics data

Anonymised product analytics events (which do not include your name, email, or username) may persist in aggregate form but are disassociated from your account upon deletion.
